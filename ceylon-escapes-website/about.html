Our story

Why we exist

Sri Lanka rewards insiders. The best guesthouse in Ella isn’t on the booking sites. The leopard trackers worth hiring are booked by name. The “4-hour drive” on the map is 7 in real life — and the roadside buffalo-curd stop that makes it worthwhile isn’t signposted.

We’re a small Colombo-based team who’ve spent years organising trips for friends-of-friends from overseas. With direct flights finally connecting our island to Australia, we’re opening that little black book properly: driver-guides we trust like family, stays we’ve slept in ourselves, and itineraries built around real conditions — not brochure weather.

No call centres. No outsourcing. The person who plans your trip is the person on WhatsApp when you land.

How we work

Four promises

Honest routing

We’ll tell you when a place is overrated, rained-out or overrun — and what to do instead. We sell good trips, not long ones.

Real prices, in AUD

Every quote is itemised in Australian dollars with nothing hidden. No booking fees, no card surcharges, no “from” prices that never materialise.

People we vouch for

Our driver-guides and trackers are long-term partners, paid fairly. Your money lands in local hands — guesthouses, family kitchens, village guides.

There till you’re home

Flight delayed, monsoon misbehaving, someone’s crook? One WhatsApp message and we’re rerouting. 24/7 means 24/7.
