Essential Sri Lanka · 8 days

The greatest-hits loop: an ancient rock fortress, the sacred city of Kandy, the world's prettiest train ride, a leopard safari, and your toes in the Indian Ocean — all with your own driver-guide.

🕐 8 days / 7 nights 🚗 Private car + chauffeur-guide 🏨 Boutique & 4★ stays 👣 Gentle pace, big variety
Day by day

The route

Negombo → Sigiriya → Kandy → Ella → Yala → Mirissa → Galle → airport. Tap a day to expand it. Want longer on the beach or an extra safari? We'll reshape it.

Your driver-guide meets you at arrivals with a cold king coconut and your local SIM. Tonight is deliberately easy: a beachside hotel in Negombo, a seafood dinner, an early night. If you land on the morning Jetstar flight, there's time for a sunset catamaran ride with the local fishermen. 🛏 Overnight: Negombo beachfront boutique
An easy morning drive into the island's ancient heart. Afternoon climb of Pidurangala Rock for the classic sunset view across to Sigiriya — the photo you've seen, taken by you. Dinner is a village cooking experience: curry from scratch, eaten under the stars. 🛏 Overnight: Sigiriya eco-lodge
Beat the heat with an early climb of Sigiriya itself — a 5th-century palace in the sky and UNESCO World Heritage site. Continue to the Dambulla cave temples, then wind up into Kandy for the evening ceremony at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. 🛏 Overnight: Kandy hills guesthouse
The Kandy–Ella line is regularly called the most beautiful train journey on Earth: tea gardens, cloud forest, waterfalls, kids waving from village platforms. We book your reserved seats and your driver meets you in Ella with your bags already checked in. 🛏 Overnight: Ella view hotel
Sunrise walk to Nine Arch Bridge to watch the train steam across, then an easy hike up Little Adam's Peak (45 min, huge reward). Afternoon free for Ella's cafés — or add a tea factory tour, cooking class, or the Ravana Falls zipline. 🛏 Overnight: Ella view hotel
Descend from the hills to the dry-zone scrub of Yala National Park — home to the world's densest leopard population, plus elephants, sloth bears, crocs and peacocks showing off. Afternoon jeep safari with an expert tracker, then a safari-camp dinner. 🛏 Overnight: Yala safari lodge
Morning drive to the south coast. In season (Dec–Apr), sunrise whale-watching at Mirissa — blue whales, the biggest animals that have ever lived. Then swim, eat and laze your way west to Galle Fort for golden-hour ramparts and dinner inside the 17th-century walls. 🛏 Overnight: Galle Fort heritage stay
One last swim, one last string-hopper breakfast, a bit of fort shopping — then the expressway back to the airport. Flying Jetstar's evening departure? We'll add a farewell seafood lunch in Negombo on the way. ✈ Fly home (or extend — most people do)
