Essential Sri Lanka · 8 days

The greatest-hits loop: an ancient rock fortress, the sacred city of Kandy, the world’s prettiest train ride, a leopard safari and your toes in the Indian Ocean — all with your own driver-guide.

8 days / 7 nights Private car + chauffeur-guide Boutique & 4★ stays
Duration8 days, 7 nights
PaceGentle — max ~3½ hrs driving a day
Best forFirst-timers & couples
WhenYear-round · whales Dec–Apr

Day by day

How the week unfolds

Want longer on the beach or an extra safari? Every day can be reshaped.

1
Day 1Touch down & exhaleAirport → Negombo · 20 min

Your driver-guide meets you at arrivals with a cold king coconut and your local SIM. Tonight is deliberately easy: a beachside hotel in Negombo, a seafood dinner, an early night. Land on the morning flight and there’s time for a sunset catamaran ride with the local fishermen.

Negombo beachfront boutique
2
Day 2Into the Cultural TriangleNegombo → Sigiriya · 3½ hrs

An easy morning drive into the island’s ancient heart. Afternoon climb of Pidurangala Rock for the classic sunset view across to Sigiriya — the photo you’ve seen, taken by you. Dinner is a village cooking experience: curry from scratch, eaten under the stars.

Sigiriya eco-lodge
3
Day 3The Lion Rock & on to KandySigiriya → Kandy · 2½ hrs

Beat the heat with an early climb of Sigiriya itself — a 5th-century palace in the sky and UNESCO World Heritage site. Continue to the Dambulla cave temples, then wind up into Kandy for the evening ceremony at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

Kandy hills guesthouse
4
Day 4That train rideKandy → Ella · scenic rail, ~6 hrs

The Kandy–Ella line is regularly called the most beautiful train journey on Earth: tea gardens, cloud forest, waterfalls and kids waving from village platforms. We book your reserved seats, and your driver meets you in Ella with your bags already checked in.

Ella view hotel
5
Day 5Nine Arch Bridge & Little Adam’s PeakElla

Sunrise walk to Nine Arch Bridge to watch the train cross, then an easy hike up Little Adam’s Peak (45 minutes, huge reward). Afternoon free for Ella’s cafés — or add a tea factory tour, a cooking class, or the Ravana Falls zipline.

Ella view hotel
6
Day 6Leopard countryElla → Yala · 2½ hrs

Descend from the hills to the dry-zone scrub of Yala National Park — home to the world’s densest leopard population, plus elephants, sloth bears, crocs and peacocks showing off. Afternoon jeep safari with an expert tracker, then a safari-camp dinner.

Yala safari lodge
7
Day 7South coast, at lastYala → Mirissa → Galle · 3 hrs

Morning drive to the south coast. In season (December–April), sunrise whale-watching at Mirissa — blue whales, the biggest animals that have ever lived. Then swim, eat and laze your way west to Galle Fort for golden-hour ramparts and dinner inside the 17th-century walls.

Galle Fort heritage stay
8
Day 8Beach morning & homeGalle → Airport · 2 hrs via expressway

One last swim, one last string-hopper breakfast, a bit of fort shopping — then the expressway back to the airport. Flying the evening departure? We’ll add a farewell seafood lunch in Negombo on the way.

Fly home (or extend — most people do)
