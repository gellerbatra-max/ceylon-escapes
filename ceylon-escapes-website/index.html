Good to know

Questions Aussies ask us

Do I need a visa?
Yes, but it’s free and takes minutes. Australians get a free 30-day tourist ETA (double entry) — apply at the official site eta.gov.lk before you fly, and avoid copycat sites that charge a “service fee”.
Is Sri Lanka safe to visit?
Tourism is booming and travellers routinely describe Sri Lankans as the friendliest people they’ve met. As with anywhere, check the latest advice on Smartraveller before you book — and you’ll have our local team on WhatsApp the whole time you’re here.
When’s the best time to go?
There’s no bad time — the two monsoons hit opposite coasts. December–April is prime on the south and west coasts; May–September is the east coast’s season (right in the Australian winter). The hills and ancient cities are good most of the year. Try the month planner above.
How much should I budget?
Less than you’d think. Our private tours start around A$185 per person per day including car, driver-guide, boutique hotels and breakfast. On the ground, a big lunch is A$5–10, a beer about A$3, and most attraction tickets A$15–50. Add international flights on top.
Why book with a local operator instead of DIY?
You can absolutely DIY Sri Lanka — but distances are deceptive (150 km can take 4 hours), hotel quality varies wildly, and the best experiences aren’t on booking sites. A private driver-guide costs less than most people expect and turns logistics into holiday. We’re on the ground, we know the owners, and we answer at 2am.
What about food, water and mozzies?
Eat everything — just stick to bottled or filtered water (your car is stocked). Bring repellent as you would for Queensland. Pharmacies are everywhere and cheap. Power plugs are UK-style Type G (plus older Type D), so pack an adapter.
