Before you fly

The free 30-day visa

Since 25 May 2026, Australian passport holders get their 30-day tourist visa (ETA) free — it used to cost US$50 per person. It’s a five-minute online form, and approval usually lands the same day.

  • 1
    Apply at the official portal — eta.gov.lk — before you fly. Beware lookalike sites that charge a “service fee”.
  • 2
    It’s a double-entry visa, so a Maldives side-hop mid-trip is allowed.
  • 3
    Staying longer than 30 days? Extensions are possible in Colombo — we’ll point you the right way.

Passport rule of thumb: at least six months’ validity from your arrival date.

Flight FAQs

Quick answers

When did Jetstar’s Sri Lanka flights start?
The first Melbourne (Tullamarine) → Colombo flight departed on 25 August 2026. The route runs three times a week, year-round, on Boeing 787 Dreamliners — Jetstar’s first South Asian route and the only low-cost direct link between Australia and Sri Lanka.
How long is the flight from Melbourne to Sri Lanka?
Around 11 hours non-stop. Compare that with 14–18+ hours door-to-door on the old one-stop routes via Singapore, KL or the Middle East.
I’m not in Melbourne — what are my options?
Connect domestically to Melbourne to keep it cheap and on one booking, or fly one-stop from your city via Singapore, KL or Bangkok. We’re happy to suggest the smoothest connections for your dates (we just don’t sell the tickets).
Should I book flights or the tour first?
Grab the flights when you see a fare you like — sale fares vanish fast. Your land itinerary is flexible: tell us your flight times and we design around them. If you’d rather lock the itinerary first, we’ll hold your quote while you watch fares.
What about surfboards or golf clubs?
Jetstar sells oversized-item allowances at booking (cheapest online, priciest at the airport). Boards travel fine — and if you’d rather not lug one, Arugam Bay and Weligama rent everything from soft-tops to step-ups for a few dollars an hour.
