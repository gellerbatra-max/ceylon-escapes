Before you fly

The free 30-day visa

Since 25 May 2026, Australian passport holders get their 30-day tourist visa (ETA) free — it used to cost US$50 per person. It's a five-minute online form, and approval usually lands the same day.

  • 1
    Apply online at the official portal — eta.gov.lk — before you fly. Beware lookalike sites that charge a "service fee".
  • 2
    It's a double-entry visa, so a Maldives side-hop mid-trip is allowed.
  • 3
    Staying longer than 30 days? Extensions are possible in Colombo — we'll point you the right way.

Passport rule of thumb: at least 6 months' validity from your arrival date.
