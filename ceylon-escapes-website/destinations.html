Where to go (and when)

Sri Lanka's trick is that its two monsoons hit opposite coasts — so there is always, always a sunny side. Here's the island, region by region, with honest advice on timing.

The places

Nine stops that earn their spot

Sigiriya & the Cultural Triangle

2 nights idealBest Apr–SepUNESCO ×3

A 200-metre rock with a 1,600-year-old palace on top, surrounded by water gardens that pre-date most of Europe's cities. Climb Sigiriya early, watch sunset from Pidurangala next door, and day-trip to the Dambulla cave temples or the ancient capitals of Polonnaruwa and Anuradhapura. Between August and October, hundreds of wild elephants gather nearby at Minneriya — one of Asia's great wildlife spectacles.

Kandy

1–2 nightsPerahera festival Jul–AugSacred city

The last royal capital, wrapped around a lake in the hills. Home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic — Buddhism's holiest shrine on the island — and, each July–August, the Esala Perahera: ten nights of fire dancers, drummers and lantern-lit processions. Time it right (the Jetstar launch does) and it's unforgettable.

Ella & the Hill Country

2 nights idealCool 18–24°CTHAT train

Arrive on the Kandy–Ella railway — six hours of tea gardens, cloud forest and waterfalls that flew past too quickly. Then: sunrise at Little Adam's Peak, the Nine Arch Bridge, tea factory tours, and café evenings with a view. The hills are Sri Lanka's answer to a completely different country.

Yala National Park

1–2 nightsBest Feb–SepLeopards

The densest leopard population on Earth, plus elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles and shameless peacocks. Dawn and dusk jeep safaris with a good tracker (ours are excellent, and we brief them on what you care about). Prefer elephants and fewer jeeps? We'll swap in Udawalawe or Wilpattu.

Galle & the South Coast

2–3 nightsBest Dec–AprUNESCO fort

A 17th-century Dutch fort city on a peninsula — ramparts at golden hour, boutique hotels in merchant houses, and the best coffee on the island. Either side stretch the southern beaches: Unawatuna, Weligama's gentle surf, palm-leaning Mirissa, and barefoot-chic Hiriketiya and Tangalle further east.

Mirissa

2 nightsWhales Dec–AprBeach bars

By day, the launch point for blue whale watching — the largest animals that have ever existed, a few kilometres offshore. By night, barefoot dinners of grilled mahi-mahi with the sand between your toes. The Coconut Tree Hill photo? That's here.

Arugam Bay

3+ nightsSurf Apr–OctAussie winter ✓

A world-top-ten right-hand point break with a proper surf-town soul — think Byron twenty years ago, at a third of the price. Main Point for the confident, Whiskey Point and Peanut Farm for learners, lagoon safaris and lagoon-side curry for everyone. Peak season lines up exactly with the Australian winter.

Trincomalee & the East

2–3 nightsBest May–SepSnorkelling

Long, quiet, white-sand bays — Uppuveli and Nilaveli — with reef snorkelling at Pigeon Island national park and sperm-whale watching in season. Shoulder-deep calm water makes it the family pick. Further south, Pasikuda's shallow bay barely gets waist-high for hundreds of metres.

Colombo

1 night, topsYear-roundFood city

Don't skip it entirely — Colombo has become a genuinely fun food city: hopper joints, crab restaurants, rooftop bars, and Pettah market's beautiful chaos. One well-planned evening on your way in or out is exactly the right dose. Your Jetstar flight lands 30 minutes up the road.
