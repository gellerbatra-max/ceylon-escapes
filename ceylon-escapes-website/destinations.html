UNESCO ×3
01 — Cultural Triangle

Sigiriya & the ancient cities

2 nights ideal Best Apr–Sep

A 200-metre rock with a 1,600-year-old palace on top, ringed by water gardens older than most of Europe’s cities. Climb Sigiriya early, watch sunset from Pidurangala next door, and day-trip to the Dambulla cave temples or the ancient capitals of Polonnaruwa and Anuradhapura. From August to October, hundreds of wild elephants gather at nearby Minneriya — one of Asia’s great wildlife spectacles.

02 — Hill country

Kandy

1–2 nights Sacred city

The last royal capital, wrapped around a lake in the hills. Home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic — the island’s holiest Buddhist shrine — and, each July–August, the Esala Perahera: ten nights of fire dancers, drummers and lantern-lit processions. Time it right and it’s unforgettable.

03 — Hill country

Ella & the tea hills

2 nights ideal Best Jan–Apr, Jul–Sep

Arrive on the Kandy–Ella railway — six hours of tea gardens, cloud forest and waterfalls that fly past too quickly. Then: sunrise at Little Adam’s Peak, the Nine Arch Bridge, tea factory tours and café evenings with a view. The hills feel like a completely different country.

04 — The deep south

Yala National Park

1–2 nights Best Feb–Aug

The densest leopard population on Earth, plus elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles and shameless peacocks. Dawn and dusk jeep safaris with a good tracker — ours are excellent, and we brief them on what you care about. Prefer elephants and fewer jeeps? We’ll swap in Udawalawe or Wilpattu. (Parts of Yala usually close for a few weeks around September–October; we plan around it.)

05 — South coast

Galle & the south coast

2–3 nights Best Dec–Apr

A 17th-century Dutch fort city on a peninsula — ramparts at golden hour, boutique hotels in merchant houses and the best coffee on the island. Either side stretch the southern beaches: Unawatuna, Weligama’s gentle surf, palm-leaning Mirissa, and barefoot-chic Hiriketiya and Tangalle further east.

06 — South coast

Mirissa

2 nights Beach bars

By day, the launch point for blue whale watching — the largest animals that have ever lived, a few kilometres offshore. By night, barefoot dinners of grilled mahi-mahi with sand between your toes. The Coconut Tree Hill photo? That’s here.

07 — East coast

Arugam Bay

3+ nights Surf Apr–Oct

A world-class right-hand point break with a proper surf-town soul — think Byron twenty years ago, at a third of the price. Main Point for the confident, Whiskey Point and Peanut Farm for learners, lagoon safaris and lagoon-side curry for everyone. Peak season lines up exactly with the Australian winter.

08 — East coast

Trincomalee & the east

2–3 nights Best May–Sep

Long, quiet, white-sand bays — Uppuveli and Nilaveli — with reef snorkelling at Pigeon Island National Park and whale watching in season. Shoulder-deep calm water makes it the family pick. Further south, Pasikuda’s shallow bay barely gets waist-high for hundreds of metres.

09 — West coast

Colombo

1 night, tops Year-round

Don’t skip it entirely — Colombo has become a genuinely fun food city: hopper joints, crab restaurants, rooftop bars and Pettah market’s beautiful chaos. One well-planned evening on your way in or out is exactly the right dose. Your flight lands about 30 minutes up the road.
